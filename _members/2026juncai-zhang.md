### 个人简介/About Me

Juncai Zhang received B.S degree in Department of Mathematics, China University of Mining and Technology, and received M.S degree in the Department of Mathematics at Shantou University under the supervision of <a href="https://www.stu.edu.cn/xxgk/dzld1/hzf.htm"><strong>Prof. Zhifeng Hao</strong></a>, and received joint training at the Shenzhen Institutes of Advanced Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, advised by <a href="https://people.ucas.edu.cn/~hzhang10"><strong>Associate Prof. Hao Zhang</strong></a>. He is currently pursuing a Ph.D. degree at the Department of Computer Science and Technology, Tongji University, where he started in 2026 and is expected to complete in 2030. He is also a student member of the IEEE, CAAI, and ORSC.

### 个人简介/About Me
Chen dekai is currently a PhD student at the School of Computer Science and Technology, Tongji University, where he is beginning his doctoral studies with an anticipated graduation in 2032. He previously completed his Bachelor of Engineering in Data Science and Big Data Technology at the same institution from 2022 to 2026. As an early-stage researcher, he remains deeply grateful for the foundational training received during his undergraduate years and approaches his doctoral work with humility, recognizing that there is still much to learn. He hopes to grow steadily through this next phase of study and to contribute meaningfully, however modestly, to the broader academic community over time.

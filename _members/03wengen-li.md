### 个人简介/About Me
Wengen Li received the B.Eng. degree and Ph.D. degree in Computer Science from Tongji University, Shanghai, China, in 2011 and 2017, respectively. He also received a dual Ph.D. degree in Computer Science from the Hong Kong Polytechnic University in 2018. He is currently an Associate Professor of the School of Computer Science and Technology at Tongji University. He is a member of China Computer Federation (CCF), a member of IEEE, and a member of ACM.

### 研究方向/Research Interests
Spatiotemporal intelligence and multimodal AI, with research outcomes enabling applications in urban computing, ocean computing, industrial intelligence, etc.

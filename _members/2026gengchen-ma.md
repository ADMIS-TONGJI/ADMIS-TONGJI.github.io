### 个人简介/About Me

Gengchen Ma received the B.Eng. degree in Software Engineering from Changzhou University in 2022 and the M.Eng. degree in Control Science and Engineering from Shanghai University of Engineering Science in 2026. He is currently pursuing the Ph.D. degree in Computer Science and Technology at Tongji University, Shanghai, China, and is expected to graduate in 2030.
